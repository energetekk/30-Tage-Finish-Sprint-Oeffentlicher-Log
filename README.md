## Why this exists

**Buildspace shut down in August 2024.** It wasn't the content people missed. It was the structure and the deadline.

The planning problem is solved. Every developer with a side project has used Claude or ChatGPT to generate an architecture, a roadmap, a task breakdown. The execution problem is not solved.

Three independent data points from three different fields say the same thing:

**1. Productivity research (METR, July 2025)**
Experienced developers working on real-world tasks were **19% slower** with AI coding tools than without. The tools that were supposed to accelerate shipping actually increased friction. More context-switching. More setup. Less finishing.

**2. Enterprise security (Dr. Karsten Nohl)**
Dr. Nohl — one of Europe's most respected security researchers — independently described the same ratio: **90% machine, 10% human — at the right moments.** Not "human in the loop everywhere." Strategic human checkpoints at the moments that matter. That's the architecture MVP Builder runs on.

**3. Career coaching (Dr. Nancy Li, PM Accelerator, Forbes Contributor)**
Dr. Li coaches developers transitioning into AI product roles. Her observation: *"most people wasn't able to make it happen, it just stays as a dream for them."* She calls it the Piano Problem: you can learn all the music theory, read every note, understand every key — and still not be able to play Mozart. Knowing how to build and actually building are different skills. AI solved the theory part. It hasn't touched the practice part.

**The Mo Gawdat counterpoint**
Mo Gawdat — former Chief Business Officer at Google X, founder of Emma AI — is frequently cited as proof that solo developers can build with AI alone. He had 12 years inside one of the best-resourced AI labs on the planet, co-founders, and $100M+ in backing. The comparison doesn't hold for a developer with 5–10 hours per week after work. Even Gawdat's own framing is honest about this: the structure and accountability came from the institution, not the tools.

MVP Builder is the structure layer that AI doesn't provide.
